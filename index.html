Craftory starts in a goblin scrapyard, with cranks, burners and pipes held together with hope. Then it climbs into wired-up crystals and living Glimmerwells you hunt, calm, jar and carry home through the storm. It's built for two friends in co-op from the first block.

Hunting a Glimmerwell

Wells are living sources of magic. They're born in storms, rain and battles, and they don't want to come home with you.

    Logistics you can watch

    Gutterpipes

    You can see the items rattling through. Pipes carry an item lane and a fluid lane. Paint daubs sort items by colour, and the Snatcher Claw pulls from chests and machines.

    Fluids

    Stack Sloshtanks to make bigger tanks. Pipes pull water from source blocks. The Gutter Boiler turns water into Pressurised Fizz, and Gasbags store it (keep fire away).

    Hurl Tubes

    Ride a tube network between named stations, or get fired from a Hurl Cannon at a Catch Net. Rides cost a little Fizz. You can't board one while carrying a jarred Well.

    Made for two

    • Crews. /craftory crew create, then invite your friend. Research, Notebook pages, Insight and tier progress are shared.
    • Ownership. Machines belong to the crew that placed them. Outsiders can't take them apart or straighten your tether stakes.
    • Two-person jobs. One player tethers and jars a Well while the other fights off its guardian and Poacher ambushes on the way home.
    • Shared readouts. Squint Goggles show a crewmate's jar Stability, and machine status at a glance.
    • Bases keep running. Machines pause when nobody's around. A fuelled Wakebell Anchor keeps an area ticking while you're out exploring.

    Get started

    Install (server)

    1. Use a Hytale server on version 0.7.4.
    2. Put craftory-core and craftory-tech in the server's mods folder. Tech needs Core.
    3. Start the server and join. Grizzlecog the goblin trader sells your first parts.

    Build from source

    git clone https://github.com/brettsaunders21/Craftory-Hytale
cd Craftory-Hytale
./gradlew setupHytaleDev   # one-time Hytale sign-in
./gradlew build
./gradlew runAllMods       # dev server with both plugins

    Your first hour

    1. Buy a Crank-a-Fizz and a Fizz Canister from Grizzlecog.
    2. Crank, store the Fizz, and run a Scorch Oven to smelt your first bars.
    3. Use the Tinker's Wrench on machines you don't know yet to take them apart and learn their Notebook pages.
    4. Find all 8 Tinkered pages, then hammer together a Junk Heart to reach Tier 2.
    5. Wait for a storm. Your first Flicker is out there.
